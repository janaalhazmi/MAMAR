## 2. Ideas Explored & Rejection Rationale

### ● Idea 1: Dual-care and coordination management website for parents of children with special needs (autism and chronic conditions).

**Strengths:** Highly targeted niche addressing fragmented care management, seamless parental collaboration through a shared daily log, and an emergency panic button displaying vital calming details instantly.

**Weaknesses:** User fatigue and burnout could hinder consistent daily data entry, and managing multiple complex medical datasets requires an ultra-simple interface to avoid adding extra stress.

**Reason for Rejection:** The core concept is already repetitive and saturated in the market compared to existing health and caregiver tracking platforms, lacking a truly disruptive unique selling proposition beyond standard features.

### ● Idea 2: Used Games Exchange Platform

**Strengths:** Provides a dedicated platform for gamers to buy, sell, and exchange used games and gaming accessories. Helps users save money by giving them access to cheaper second-hand games. Makes it easier for gamers to find specific games and connect with other gamers. Encourages reusing games instead of leaving them unused.

**Weaknesses:** Requires a large number of active users to provide enough games and offers. Trust and safety can be an issue when buying used products from individuals. Users may face problems with the condition or compatibility of used games and devices. Competing with established marketplaces would make it difficult to attract users.

**Reason for Rejection:** The concept is already available through established platforms such as Haraj and other classified marketplaces, where users can already sell and buy used games, consoles, and accessories. Therefore, the idea did not provide enough differentiation or a strong unique value proposition compared with existing platforms.

## 3. Selected MVP Concept

### ● Selected Idea Summary:
○ MAMAR is a GIS-based B2B platform that connects contractors with traffic control service providers to manage temporary traffic control operations for construction and roadwork projects. Contractors can define their work zone on an interactive map, request traffic control services, and coordinate the implementation of temporary traffic elements such as road diversions, barriers, and traffic signs. Service providers can manage requests and document the actual field implementation using geographic locations and site information.

### ● The Problem It Solves:
○ Construction and roadwork projects often require temporary traffic control measures, such as road diversions, barriers, traffic signs, and temporary traffic routes around work zones. Coordinating these services between contractors and traffic control service providers can involve multiple parties and requires clear location-based information. MAMAR addresses this problem by providing a centralized GIS-based platform where contractors can define their work zone, request the required traffic control services, connect with suitable service providers, and document the implementation geographically.

### ● Target Audience:
○ Contractors: Construction, infrastructure, and roadwork contractors who require temporary traffic control services around their project work zones.

○ Traffic Control Service Providers: Companies that provide and implement temporary traffic control services, such as road diversions, barriers, traffic signs, and temporary traffic routes.

### ● Key Features:

**1. Interactive Work Zone Map:**  
Contractors can mark the exact project area and visualize the work zone on an interactive map.

**2. Service Request & Provider Selection:**  
Contractors can specify the required traffic control services and select an available service provider for the project.

**3. Project Status & Field Documentation:**  
Both parties can follow the request status, while service providers record completed field work and update project information.

### ● Rationale & Alignment:

○ Feasibility: The project is achievable within the available timeframe using our team’s technical skills in frontend and backend development, database management, API integration, and GIS. The MVP has a well-defined scope that allows the team to build and integrate the core system components progressively.

○ Innovation & Impact: MAMAR brings contractors and traffic control service providers into one digital workflow, simplifying service coordination and improving visibility throughout the process. The platform combines service management with location-based capabilities to provide a more organized and practical approach to temporary traffic control operations.

○ Scalability: The system is designed to support future growth by allowing new services and features to be added without changing the core workflow. Future versions could include real-time tracking, automated notifications, payment integration, advanced project dashboards, and additional traffic management services.
